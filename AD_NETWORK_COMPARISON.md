# Ad Network Comparison for TextWonder

**Your Current Stats:**
- 10K pageviews/month (still growing)
- Niche: Utility tools (text, PDF, dev, calculators)
- Location: Global (India 24%, USA 6%, UK 2%, etc.)
- Audience: Developers, students, professionals
- Ad Policy: Privacy-first focus

---

## 🏆 Quick Recommendation

**For your stage:** Start with **Google AdSense** (easiest entry), then upgrade to **Mediavine** once you hit 25K pageviews/month.

**Why:** AdSense approves faster (5-7 days), requires only 10K pageviews, and maintains your privacy brand. Mediavine pays 3-4x more but requires 25K pageviews.

- ✅ Fast approval

### 2. **Mediavine** ⭐⭐ UPGRADE TARGET

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Eligibility** | ❌ 25K+ pageviews/month (you need 2.5x more traffic) |
| **Approval Time** | 2-3 weeks |

| **Payment Frequency** | Monthly (1-15th) |

**Pros:**
- ✅ 5-10x higher CPM than AdSense
- ✅ Dedicated account manager (premium support)

- ✅ Better ad formats (responsive, minimally intrusive)

**Cons:**
- ❌ High traffic barrier (25K+ pageviews/month)
- ❌ Competitive approval process (not guaranteed)
- ❌ Longer approval (2-3 weeks)
- ❌ Requires 90+ days of traffic history
- ❌ Account manager might push more aggressive ad placements

**Timeline to eligibility:** 2-3 months (if you grow to ~850 pageviews/day)  
**Est. Revenue at 25K pageviews/mo:** $500-750/month

## ⚠️ **Things to Avoid**

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use Propeller Ads (ruins brand for minimal revenue)
- Join multiple ad networks on same pages (violates policies)
- Switch too frequently (disrupts earnings patterns)
- Optimize for ads over UX (AdSense will suspend you)
- Use fake traffic or bot clicks (instant ban)

✅ **DO:**
- Monitor your CPM weekly
- Block low-paying ad categories
- Test placements for 2 weeks before major changes
- Keep detailed records of revenue
- Apply to premium networks 1 month before eligibility
